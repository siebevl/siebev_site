Slow birding morning, with many birds heard but left unseen (annoying close calls: bramling, common linnet and redwing). Nice encounter with three lovely roe deer, though. Also a soaring sparrowhawk over De Afgraving (not pictured, instead, look at the cute long-tailed tit).
